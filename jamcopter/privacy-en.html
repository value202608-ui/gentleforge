칼퇴특공대 (JAMCOPTER) Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 28, 2026

칼퇴특공대 ("JAMCOPTER", "the app") is made by Gentle Forge (점잖은대장간, "the developer"), a sole proprietorship in the Republic of Korea. This policy explains, truthfully, what information the app handles.

1. Information we collect

The app has no sign-up and no login, and it does not collect names, contact details, email addresses, precise location, photos or contacts. The developer runs no servers and uses no usage-statistics or analytics tools. The developer receives no information about you. The only communication the app starts with anything outside your device is with the stores, for payments, purchase checks and the rating prompt (section 3).

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is stored only on your device so you can continue playing. It is never sent to the developer.

  • Game progress: stars per site, in-game treasury and upgrades, rank, achievements, daily orders, totals (such as arrests)
  • In-game counters: for example, how many times the Full Member card was shown
  • Settings: sound, music, screen shake, vibration, control scheme
  • The site you picked as "my commute": a name chosen from a list, not location data. You can skip it.
  • Whether you bought Full Member or cosmetics: the result the store confirmed, remembered so that buyers keep access while offline.

This information is deleted when you uninstall the app. If you turned on your device's backup (Android backup, iCloud backup and so on), the operating system may include app data in your account's backup under the policies of its provider (Google, Apple).

Photo-mode pictures and highlight videos (a few seconds of a big moment from a run) are made only on your device and are never sent to the developer. A video contains only the game screen and game sound; the microphone is not used. It leaves your device only when you tap share, through your device's share sheet to the app you choose. The file made for sharing is kept in the app's temporary folder and deleted at the next share.

3. Payments and the stores

  • Full Member and cosmetics (items such as finales and parachutes that only change how things look) are bought through Google Play Billing or Apple App Store In-App Purchase. Card numbers and other payment details are handled by the store; the developer never receives them.
  • To check purchases, restores and ownership, the app talks to the store on your device (Google Play, App Store). Information that the store and its payment components (Google Play Billing Library, Apple StoreKit) process or send to the store in doing so, for handling payments and diagnosing errors, follows the Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and the Apple Privacy Policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).
  • After a purchase the app may ask the store to show its rating prompt. Ratings are handled by the store.

4. Processors and international transfer

The developer does not sell or share personal information with third parties. There is no personal information that the developer entrusts to anyone else for processing or transfers abroad. Payments are handled directly by the stores (Google, Apple) under their own policies, as in section 3.

5. Permissions

  • Internet: store payments and purchase checks.
  • Vibration (Android): for in-game haptic feedback.
  • Add to Photos (iPhone): only when you choose Save in the share sheet, the app adds the picture or video to your Photos library. It does not read your other photos.
  • The app does not use location, camera, microphone or contacts permissions.

6. Children

The app is not designed for children under 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14. The app does not ask for your age.

7. Your rights and how to refuse

  • You can erase the game data on your device by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in your device settings.
