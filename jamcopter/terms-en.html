4. Free sites and Full Member

  • Free: the five Chapter 1 sites (Geumcheon, Seonam, Yangjae, Sadang, Hannam) can be played to the end (all three stars), with no time limits or energy. One "wanted site of the week", marked "이번 주 무료" on the map, is also free for that week; it changes weekly.
  • Full Member ("정식 대원" in the app and in the store — a one-time, non-consumable in-app purchase) gives you the following.
  • Benefit one: the Chapter 2–5 sites and the Seoul-area sites added in updates, with no further payment. The timing and number of added sites may change with development. Sites outside the Seoul area may be offered as separate products.
  • Benefit two: in-game values earned in a run (tax, on-the-spot reward, prevention reward, fever bonus) go into your treasury doubled, starting from the next settlement after purchase. This does not apply to stars, commendations, promotions or daily-order rewards.
  • Regular weapons and the Last-Train ICBM, with their upgrades and awakenings, are earned by playing the free sites.
  • The Chapter 2–4 ultimates (Clock-Out Sub, Boss's Cane, Unidentified Flying Manager) unlock when you earn a star on a site of that chapter; their upgrades and awakenings are also earned by playing. Chapters 2–4 are Full Member sites; in a week when one of them is the wanted site of the week, free players can earn stars there too. No weapon unlocks by payment alone.
  • Cosmetics (one-time, non-consumable in-app purchases — Armory → Shop): they change only how things look in a run and in share clips, such as the scene at the end of a sweep (the finale) or the pattern of the ejection parachute of an arrested driver. They have no effect on weapons, tax, stars, records or commendations. They are sold at fixed prices (no loot boxes), and some can also be earned with commendations. The starter pack (one finale + one parachute) can be bought once per account and is not shown if you already own any of its items. You can equip or remove owned cosmetics in the shop.
  • The price is the amount the store shows on the purchase screen (including VAT). The price may change; existing purchasers are not charged again.
  • One-run cosmetic trial: once a day in the shop, you can play your next run with a finale you do not own yet. There are no loot boxes and no paid currency.
