1. 기기 안에 저장하는 것

게임은 아래 내용을 기기 안의 앱 저장 공간에만 저장합니다. 어느 것도 기기 밖으로 보내지 않습니다.

  • 게임 진행: 딴 별, 모은 별가루, 강화 단계, 새 세상에 세운 것, 만난 하늘(낮 · 노을 · 밤), 찾은 귀한 손님
  • 구매 표시: '서울 전부 열기'를 샀는지, 언제 열렸는지, 어느 휴대폰 체제(안드로이드 또는 iOS)에서 열렸는지. 인터넷이 없을 때도 산 동네를 열어 두려고 저장합니다.
  • 설정: 배경 음악 켜기 · 끄기, 고른 효과 모양, 이미 본 안내(처음 한 번만 보여 주는 설명을 다시 띄우지 않으려고)

이 기록은 앱을 지우거나 기기 설정에서 앱 데이터를 지우면 함께 지워집니다. 산 것은 게임 안의 「구매 복원」으로 다시 열 수 있습니다. 게임 진행은 우리에게 없으므로 되살려 드릴 수 없습니다.

기기 백업: 안드로이드 판은 이 기록을 Google 백업과 새 휴대폰으로 옮기기에 넣지 않도록 해 두었습니다. iOS 판에서는 기기의 iCloud 백업을 켜 두셨다면, 운영체제가 이 기록을 보호자님의 iCloud 백업에 넣을 수 있습니다. 그 백업은 Apple이 관리하며, 우리는 볼 수 없습니다.

2. 모으지 않는 것

  • 이름, 생일, 이메일, 전화번호, 주소 같은 개인정보
  • 위치 정보, 사진, 카메라, 마이크, 연락처
  • 광고 식별자, 기기 고유 번호
  • 게임을 어떻게 하는지에 대한 기록(사용 통계, 분석, 오류 보고)

게임에는 채팅이나 다른 사람과 어울리는 기능이 없습니다. 쿠키 같은 추적 장치도 쓰지 않습니다.

게임은 휴대폰의 언어 설정을 읽어 한국어 또는 영어로 보여 줍니다. 이 값은 기기 안에서만 쓰고, 저장하거나 보내지 않습니다.

안드로이드 판

  • 인터넷 접근: 앱의 기본 틀(Capacitor)에 들어 있는 권한입니다. 게임 코드는 바깥 서버에 연결하지 않습니다.

결제를 위해 앱에는 Google이 만든 Google Play 결제 라이브러리(와 그것이 쓰는 Google 부품)가 들어 있고, 이 부품은 휴대폰의 Google Play와 통신합니다. 이 부품이 Google과 주고받는 정보는 Google 개인정보처리방침을 따르며, 우리에게는 오지 않습니다.

iOS 판

5. 아이들의 개인정보

이 게임은 어린이도 즐길 수 있게 만들었습니다. 어느 판이든, 아이를 포함해 누구에게서도 개인정보를 모으지 않습니다. 그래서 아이에게 개인정보를 묻지 않습니다.

  • 한국: 개인정보 보호법은 만 14세 미만 아동의 개인정보를 처리하려면 법정대리인(보호자)의 동의를 받도록 합니다(제22조의2). 게임은 아이의 개인정보를 받지 않으므로, 아이에게 개인정보나 동의를 요구하지 않습니다.
  • 미국: COPPA(아동 온라인 개인정보 보호법)는 13세 미만 아이에게서 개인정보를 모으기 전에 보호자의 확인 가능한 동의를 받도록 합니다. 게임은 아이에게서 개인정보를 모으지 않습니다.
  • 유럽연합 · 영국: GDPR은 아이의 개인정보를 더 두텁게 보호합니다. 나라에 따라 13~16세 미만 아이가 온라인 서비스에 동의할 때 보호자 동의가 필요합니다. 게임은 플레이어의 개인정보를 처리하지 않으므로 동의를 묻지 않습니다.

게임 안의 보호자 확인은 아이가 혼자 앱 밖의 웹 페이지로 나가지 않게 막는 장치입니다. 개인정보를 모으려고 받는 동의 절차가 아닙니다.

이 항목은 우리가 게임을 어떻게 만들었는지 알려 드리는 설명입니다. 어떤 기관의 인증을 받았다는 뜻은 아닙니다.

Gulp Roll Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 November 2026

Gentle Forge (점잖은대장간, "we") makes the mobile game Gulp Roll (Korean title: 꿀꺽 블랙홀, "the game"). This policy applies in the same way to the Google Play (Android) version and the App Store (iOS) version. We wrote it in short, plain words so parents can read it quickly.

At a glance

  • There is no sign-up and no login.
  • We do not collect personal information such as names, birthdays, contact details, location, photos or device identifiers.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics tools and no third-party tracking tools.
  • Game progress is saved only on the device. It is never sent to us.
  • Purchases are handled by the store you got the game from (Google Play or Apple's App Store). Our app never receives payment details such as card numbers.

1. What the game saves on the device

The game saves the following only in the app's own storage on the device. None of it leaves the device.

  • Progress: stars earned, stardust collected, upgrade levels, what has been built in the New World, skies seen (day, sunset, night) and rare guests found.
  • Purchase flag: whether "Unlock all of Seoul" has been bought, when it was unlocked, and on which phone system (Android or iOS). We save this so purchased neighbourhoods stay open without an internet connection.
  • Settings: background music on or off, the chosen visual effect, and which tips have already been shown (so that first-time explanations are not shown again).

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in the device settings. Purchases can be unlocked again with "Restore purchases" inside the game. We do not hold your game progress, so we cannot bring it back.

Device backup: the Android version is set so that this data is left out of Google backup and out of transfers to a new phone. On the iOS version, if iCloud backup is turned on for the device, the operating system may include this data in your iCloud backup. That backup is managed by Apple, and we cannot see it.

2. What the game does not collect

  • Personal information such as name, birthday, email address, phone number or home address.
  • Location, photos, camera, microphone or contacts.
  • Advertising IDs or other unique device identifiers.
  • Records of how the game is played (usage statistics, analytics or crash reports).

The game has no chat and no features for talking to other people. It does not use cookies or similar tracking technology.

The game reads the phone's language setting to show Korean or English. This value is used only on the device; it is not saved or sent anywhere.

3. App permissions and payment components

Android version

  • Internet access: included by the app framework (Capacitor). The game's own code does not connect to any outside server.
  • Billing and network state: used by Google Play in-app purchases.
  • Vibration: used for vibration feedback during play.

The game does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photo and file permissions.

To take payments, the app includes the Google Play Billing Library made by Google (and the Google components it uses). These components talk to Google Play on the phone. Information they exchange with Google is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and is not sent to us.

iOS version

The game does not ask for location, camera, microphone, contacts or photo permissions. To take payments, it uses Apple's payment feature built into Apple devices (App Store in-app purchase). Information this feature exchanges with Apple is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy and is not sent to us.

4. Purchases

  • The first two neighbourhoods (Yeouido and Sangam) can be played without paying. The other Seoul neighbourhoods are unlocked by one one-time in-app purchase, "Unlock all of Seoul". See our Terms of Service for details.
  • Payment is handled by the store you got the game from: Google Play for the Android version, Apple (the App Store) for the iOS version. Our app never receives card numbers, bank details or payment account information.
  • The app uses the purchase result from the store only to check whether this item is owned, and then opens the neighbourhoods. We do not run a server to check purchases, and the app does not send the purchase result off the device.
  • When the app starts, it automatically asks the store whether the item is already owned (on Android, also when you come back to the app). This is so that a purchase opens, and a refunded purchase is locked again.
  • The store gives us, as the seller, sales records (from Google Play, things like order number, item, amount and country; from Apple, sales reports with things like country and amount). We use these only to handle refund questions and to keep tax and accounting records, and keep them for the period the law requires (under Korean e-commerce law, records of contracts and payments are kept for 5 years).
  • Checking who is paying is done by the store at the time of payment. The grown-up check in the app (a multiplication question written in words) is only in front of links that leave the app (the privacy policy and terms).
  • Information that Google or Apple handles during payment is covered by that company's own privacy policy, and it does not come to us: Google Privacy Policy · Apple Privacy Policy

5. Children's privacy

We made this game so that children can enjoy it too. In every version, the game collects no personal information from anyone, including children, so it never asks children for personal information.

  • Korea: the Personal Information Protection Act requires consent from a legal guardian before personal information of a child under 14 is processed (Article 22-2). The game does not receive children's personal information, so it never asks a child for personal information or consent.
  • United States: COPPA (the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) requires verifiable parental consent before personal information is collected from a child under 13. The game does not collect personal information from children.
  • European Union and United Kingdom: the GDPR gives children's personal data extra protection. Depending on the country, a parent must consent when a child under 13 to 16 gives consent to an online service. The game does not process players' personal data, so it does not ask for consent.

The grown-up check in the game is there to stop children from leaving the app for a web page on their own. It is not a consent step for collecting personal information.
